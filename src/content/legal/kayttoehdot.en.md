## 2. The service

Pilke arranges dates. It is not a messaging app and not a personal ads board.

- **You cannot send messages to other users.** There is no chat in the service.
- **You choose who to invite.** We suggest candidates to you based on gender, age, your
  preferences, your date area and your calendar.
- **An invitation comes with times and venues already set.** You answer it by choosing the
  final time and venue.
- **Pilke provides predetermined, public venues.** Users cannot propose venues of their own.
- **You meet face to face** at the agreed place at the agreed time.

Pilke is free of charge, and nothing in the service is for sale: visibility, promotion and a
better place among suggestions cannot be bought. We cannot guarantee that you will get a
date, that candidates will be available to you, or that your invitation will be answered.

## 6. Safety in the service

**The safety of our users comes first**, and we work to protect it by the means set out
below:

- **A trusted contact and the safety button.** When you press the safety button, we send a
  text message containing your nickname to the person whose number you have saved. The
  message contains no position, no venue and nothing about the person you are meeting, and
  that person is not told you pressed it. When you save somebody's number for the safety
  button, it is your responsibility that they know about it and accept it; the number is
  their data, not yours. The safety button is not an emergency number: when you need help
  now, call 112.
- **Sharing your location at the venue**, limited as set out in section 3 of the
  [privacy policy](/en/tietosuoja).
- **A safety report** you can file after a date. Only Pilke staff can see it.
- **A block.** When you file a safety report after a date, the two of you are never
  suggested to each other again, and the other person is not told why.
- **Predetermined, public venues.** Dates are arranged only at venues chosen by Pilke, and
  users cannot propose other venues.

We do not, however, verify identities or run background checks, and we cannot guarantee that
another user is who they say they are. Nor do we supervise meetings or attend them. Be as
careful as you would be meeting any stranger: tell somebody where you are going, and leave if
it does not feel right.

## 7. Feedback and safety reports

After a date we ask how it went for the two of you. You answer by picking an option, and your
answer concerns that date partner. They cannot see your answer.

The last question is an exception. It asks whether you would like to meet again and whether
your phone number may be given to the other person. If you both want to exchange numbers, we
pass them on, and the other person then sees that your answer was yes. Otherwise we tell them
nothing about your answer. The answer is final and cannot be taken back, because the other
person may have seen and saved your number the moment you answered. See section 5 of the
[privacy policy](/en/tietosuoja).

A safety report is free text. It is never shown to the other user. Write down what happened.
The report is kept and may lead to action concerning the other user, so knowingly filing a
false report is a breach of these terms.

not turning up. A restriction lasts no longer than 30 days.

You can ask for a decision to be looked at again by writing to the address in section 1. A
person will then review it.

We may also restrict your use or end the agreement if you break these terms, if what you are
doing puts another user's safety at risk, or if the law requires it. We will tell you why,
unless doing so would put another user at risk. In a serious case a restriction can take
effect immediately; otherwise you will first be given the chance to put things right.
