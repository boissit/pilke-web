Creating a profile is one flow, and it ends with you opening your first set of candidates. You can stop part way and carry on later.

## Welcome

![Pilke's opening screen, with the buttons Start and I already have an account](shot:onboarding-tervetuloa)

If you are new, press **Start**.

If you already have an account, press **I already have an account**. Give your phone number and we text you a one-time code. There is no password.

## Terms and etiquette

![The Terms and privacy screen, with Etiquette below it](shot:onboarding-ehdot)

This screen says briefly what Pilke needs from you and who sees it. **Read the terms of use** and **Read the privacy policy** open the full documents.

Below that is the **Etiquette**: answer your invitations, turn up, and cancel as soon as you know you cannot come.

Press **I accept** once you have read them.

## Creating your profile

![The Creating your profile screen](shot:onboarding-profiilin-luonti)

A profile is your basic details, the personality quiz and what you are looking for on a date. Press **Start**.

## Contact details and the code

![Contact details, with fields for an email address and a phone number](shot:onboarding-yhteystiedot)

1. Enter your **Email address** and **Phone number**.
2. Press **Continue**. A confirmation code arrives by text message.
3. Type the code into **Confirmation code** and press **Continue**.

The code works for {{login.code_minutes}} minutes. If it does not arrive, press **Send a new code**.

Once the code is confirmed you get {{petals.signup_bonus}} petals, which is enough for your first set of candidates.

## Your details

![Your details: preferred name, date of birth and gender](shot:onboarding-omat-tiedot)

Give your **Preferred name**, **Date of birth** and **Gender**. Other people see your preferred name.

Pilke is only for people aged {{profile.min_age}} or over.

## Profile photo

![The Upload a profile photo screen](shot:onboarding-profiilikuva)

Press **Choose a photo** and pick one from your phone. It has to be a photo of you, and you have to be recognisable in it.

## Personality and the story test

![The introduction to the personality section](shot:onboarding-persoonallisuus)

Next come the personality questions, told as a story. Press **Start**.

![The first question of the story test and its options](shot:onboarding-tarinatesti)

There are {{profile.story_questions}} questions, one at a time. Choose the option closest to what you would do, and press **Continue**. Each answer is saved as you go.

You can take the test again later on the Questions page.

## Who you are looking for

Answer **What do you expect from a date?** Then choose the **Genders of matches** you are open to (one or more), a **Minimum age** and a **Maximum age**.

These decide who can be suggested to you, and they work in both directions: you are only shown people whose own wishes you fit as well.

## Languages, areas and things to do

![The languages question](shot:onboarding-kielet)

Choose the languages you could hold a date in. Anybody suggested to you shares at least one of them.

Under **Where could you meet?**, tap the map and choose your areas. Date venues are only suggested inside the areas you choose. Under the map you can see how many venues those areas hold. If they hold none, choose an area with dots on the map.

![What would you like to do on a date? and its options](shot:onboarding-treffiaktiviteetit)

Under **What would you like to do on a date?**, choose at least {{profile.min_categories}}.

## Date times

Last, you mark in the calendar the times when you could go on a date.

1. Press **Mark times**.
2. Press and hold the grid, then drag, to make a marking.
3. Press **Done**, then **Continue**.

You need at least one {{calendar.min_slot_hours}}-hour time before you can move on. The [Calendar guide](/en/guide/kalenteri) covers the rest.

## Your first set

With your profile done, you see your petals. Press **Send an invitation!** and Pilke looks for candidates for you. The petals are spent at this point, not when the invitation itself goes out. The [Candidates guide](/en/guide/ehdokkaat) takes it from there.
