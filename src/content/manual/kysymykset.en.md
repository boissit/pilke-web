Questions is the **Questions** tab in the bar at the bottom. Nothing on it is required.

## The Questions page

![The Questions page: the personality quiz and the list of question sets](shot:kysymykset#retake-story-test,question-set-0)

At the top is **The personality quiz** (1): the story test you took while creating your profile. Below it are the question sets (2). A set you have finished has a tick.

If there are no sets yet, the page says *No question sets have been published yet.*

## Answering a question set

![A question set, open](shot:kysymyssarja#question-0,save-survey)

1. Tap a set.
2. Answer the questions (1).
3. Press **Save** (2). The app confirms: *Answers saved.*

You can come back to a set later. Your earlier answers are already filled in, and you can change them.

## Retaking the personality quiz

1. On the Questions page, tap **The personality quiz**.
2. The app asks **Start over?**. Press **Start over**.
3. Answer the questions. Your earlier choices are already selected, so you can simply confirm them.

Each answer is saved as you give it and replaces the old one, even if you stop part way.

## What the answers are used for

![The Questions page explanation, open](shot:popover-surveys)

Your personality quiz answers affect the order candidates are shown in. They never rule anybody out.

Answers to the question sets do not affect who is suggested to you at the moment. They help us get to know you. See [How candidates are chosen](/en/guide/ehdokkaiden-valinta).
