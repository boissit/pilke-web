Pilke does not ask where you live and does not track where you are. On the day of a date, only what you choose to share is shared, and only with the person it concerns.

## What the person you meet sees

Before the date they see your preferred name, age and photo, and the times and places you have in common. They do not see your phone number.

After the date they do not see your feedback. Phone numbers are only swapped if you both choose that in your feedback. See [Feedback after a date](/en/guide/palaute).

## Showing your position on the map

Your position is only shown if you press **Show my position** and confirm it.

- **When.** Only from {{sharing.lead_minutes}} minutes before the start of the date until {{sharing.trail_minutes}} minutes after, {{sharing.window_minutes}} minutes in all. Before that Pilke will not accept a position at all, so nobody can locate you at home.
- **Where.** Only within about {{sharing.radius_m}} metres of the venue. A position sent from further away is refused.
- **Who.** Only the person you are meeting. Your trusted person does not see it.
- **How long.** Your position is kept on Pilke's server only so that it can be shown to them. It is deleted as soon as you stop showing it, and otherwise as soon as the time is up. At the same time Pilke deletes its record of the notice that told them you had arrived.

A position shown on the map is used for nothing else. No compensation, no cooldown and no no-show report depends on it.

You can stop at any time with **Stop showing my position**.

## Reporting that they did not turn up

When you say the other person did not turn up, your phone's location is checked to see whether you are at the venue. It is not stored and not shown to anyone. Only your own position is ever checked, never theirs. See [Not turning up](/en/guide/saapumatta-jaaminen).

## The safety button

The safety button texts your trusted person and nobody else. The message has your name and asks them to get in touch.

It does not contain your location, the venue, the time, or anything about the person you are meeting, and they are not told you pressed it. Pilke keeps a record that you pressed it.

## Your phone's calendar

If you let Pilke show your phone's calendar, it is read only on the phone. Nothing from your calendar goes to Pilke.

If you let Pilke add your dates to your calendar, the entry names the person you are meeting and the venue. Anybody who can see that calendar can see it, in Google for example. The switches are in [Settings](/en/guide/asetukset).

## After the date

Your feedback is not shown to the person you met. If you make a safety report in your feedback, only Pilke's staff see it.
