## What other people can see

| What | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| Your preferred name, age and photo, and the times and places you share | Your matches, or the person you invite | You're one of their matches, or in an invite |
| Your preferred name and phone number | Your date | You both chose to share your number in your feedback |
| Your location on a map, for {{sharing.window_minutes}} minutes | Your date | Around the start of a date, if you choose to share your location |

That's everything other people can see about you. Nobody but you can see your email address or your safety contact's number. Your date doesn't see your feedback. If Pilke adds your dates to your own calendar, such as Google or Apple Calendar, anyone who can see that calendar can see the entry. See [Your free times](/en/guide/vapaat-ajat).

## Notifications

![Notification settings: each notification can come to your phone, your email or both](shot:asetukset-ilmoitukset#notification-new_date-push,notification-new_date-email)

For each notification, you choose whether it comes to your phone (1), your email (2), both or neither. **New invites**, for example, tells you when someone invites you on a date. Email works once your address is confirmed.

Three notifications always come to your phone, because they're only useful on time:

- **Your date's starting**: you can now share your location, {{sharing.lead_minutes}} minutes before the start.
- **When your date arrives**: your date has shared their location at the venue.
- **Cancelled dates**: without it, you'd go and wait for someone who isn't coming.

The empty calendar reminder comes on Sundays at about {{notifications.calendar_nudge_hour}}:00, if you haven't marked anything for the week ahead.

## Your account

![Account open: Sign out, Sign out on all devices and Delete account](shot:asetukset-tili#sign-out-everywhere,delete-account)

You sign in with a one-time code sent to your phone number, so there's no password. Signing in is per device, and it lasts {{login.session_days}} days from when you last used the app.

**Sign out on all devices** (1) signs you out everywhere, including this device. Use it if your phone is lost or you've lent it to someone.

**Delete account** (2) deletes your account straight away, and it can't be undone:

- Your name, photo, contact details, preferences, calendar and answers are deleted at once.
- Upcoming dates are cancelled, and the other person is told. If they sent the invite, they get their sparks back. A date that's already started is left as it is.
- Invites you sent are withdrawn and invites you received are declined. Whoever sent one gets their sparks back.
- Past dates, the feedback given on them and safety reports are kept. Where your name was, people see *Deleted user*.

You can sign up again later with the same number, but you'll start from an empty account. How long each thing is kept is in the [privacy policy](/en/tietosuoja).

## Feedback

**Give feedback** is at the bottom of Settings, below the sections. The form has three boxes: **What works?**, **What doesn't work?** and **Anything else**. You can fill in one of them or all three, and the feedback comes straight to us. If you choose **I'd like a reply**, we'll reply to your email address or phone number.
