You can give feedback as soon as the date ends, {{date.length_hours}} hour after the start time. If you haven't answered, you get a reminder the next day at about {{feedback.prompt_hour}}:00, if reminders are on. You can give feedback once.

![Date feedback: your date, when it was, and the first question](shot:feedback)

The top shows which date it's about. After the first question, the form branches, and we ask slightly different questions depending on how the date went.

- **Well.** We ask what you liked about your date, whether the place was good for a date, and whether you'd like to meet them again.
- **OK.** We ask what you liked about your date and what you'd have liked more of. Then we ask about the venue and whether you'd like to meet them again.
- **Badly.** We ask first whether you felt safe on the date. If you answer *Yes*, we ask what you'd have liked more of, and then about the venue. You can read more about the other answers on the [Safety](/en/guide/turvallisuus) page.
- **The date didn't happen.** We ask what happened. See below.

Answer each question and press **Continue**.

*I liked these things about my date* and *I'd have liked more of these in my date* offer the same choices, such as *Personality*, *Humour*, *Where they are in life* and *Being on the same wavelength*. Pick at least one, or as many as you like.

Your feedback is sent when you answer the last question, and you see *Thanks for your answers.* **Done** takes you back to the Dates page, where you can see the sparks you got.

- **What happens to your answers.** We use them to understand both you and your date better, so we can offer you both better matches for future dates.
- **Sparks.** Once you've filled in your feedback, you get {{sparks.per_date}} sparks. You and your date each get yours for your own feedback. See [Sparks](/en/guide/kipinat).

## Meeting again

If you answered *Well* or *OK*, the last question is *Would you like to meet your date again?* The answers are *Yes, and they can have my phone number*, *Yes, but only through Pilke* and *No*. What happens depends on what you both say:

- **You both share your number.** You swap phone numbers. You're both told, and the number shows up in **Past dates**.
- **You both say yes, but at least one of you only through Pilke.** No numbers are swapped, but you can become matches for each other again.
- **Otherwise** nothing is shared, and neither of you finds out what the other answered.

To the other person, saying no looks the same as not answering.

## If the date didn't happen

If you choose *The date didn't happen*, we ask what happened.

- **My date didn't turn up.** The Pilke team is told, and your feedback earns you {{sparks.per_date}} sparks. Your date gets a time-out only from a report you make at the venue during the date. See [The day of your date](/en/guide/treffipaiva#if-your-date-doesnt-turn-up).
- **I couldn't make it.** You can tell us more about what happened, but not turning up doesn't earn sparks. Next time, cancel the date as soon as you know you can't make it, so your date's time is freed up. See [Cancelling and time-outs](/en/guide/peruminen-ja-jaahy).

## Past dates

![The bottom of the Dates page and the Past dates row](shot:date-history-open#open-date-history)

**Past dates** (1), the last row on the Dates page, shows the dates you've been on, newest first. The list leaves out cancelled dates, and dates where someone reported from the venue that the other didn't turn up.

![Past dates: dates you've been on and a phone number you swapped](shot:date-history#date-history-row-number)

A row shows your date's photo and name, and when and where you met. If you swapped numbers, the number is on the row (1). Tap the number to text it. Tap anywhere else on the row to copy it. If your date has deleted their account, the row says *Deleted user*.

## If something went wrong

If you felt unsafe on the date, or want to tell us how your date behaved, tell us in your feedback. See [Safety](/en/guide/turvallisuus).
