An invitation comes with its times and places already in it. Whoever receives it picks one time and one place, and the date is set. It has to be answered within {{invitation.answer_hours}} hours.

Sending one is covered in the [Candidates guide](/en/guide/ehdokkaat).

## An invitation you sent

![A sent invitation: the times you offered and the place](shot:sent#waiting-explainer,withdraw-invitation-open)

Your invitation is on the Dates page under **Date requests you have sent:**. Tap the card to open it.

It shows **The times you offered:** and the place. If you offered more than one place, it says **They choose the place**. The **i** beside the title (1) says how long the answer can take.

One of three things happens next:

- **They accept.** The date moves to **Agreed dates:**.
- **They decline.** You get {{petals.refund}} petals back.
- **Nobody answers within {{invitation.answer_hours}} hours.** The invitation lapses by itself and you get {{petals.refund}} petals back.

You are notified of each one, if those notifications are on in Settings.

To take the invitation back before they answer, press **Withdraw the invitation** (2) and confirm.

The petals you spent are not returned, and you cannot look for new candidates for the next {{cooldown.withdrawal_hours}} hours. See [Cooldowns](/en/guide/jaahdytys).

## An invitation you received

![A received invitation: times, places, and the Decline and Agreed! buttons](shot:invitation#timeslot-0,venue-0,invitation-accept,invitation-decline)

A new invitation is on the Dates page under **Date invitations you have received:**. Tap the card.

1. Under **Choose a time:**, pick one time (1).
2. If there is more than one place, pick one under **Choose a place:** (2). Tapping the map shows the place full screen, with a route there.
3. Press **Agreed!** (3).

That is the date agreed. It moves to **Agreed dates:** on the Dates page, and the other person is told.

If you have not made up your mind, press **Decide later** and the invitation waits on the Dates page. If you still have not answered, you get a reminder about {{invitation.expiry_warning_hours}} hours before it lapses.

If you would rather not go, press **Decline** (4), then **Decline** again to confirm.

Declining costs you nothing and needs no reason. It is a kinder answer than silence, too: the other person gets their petals back straight away.

Answering an invitation costs nothing, whichever way you answer.

## Once a date is agreed

The agreed hour is held in both your calendars. As far as Pilke is concerned the date lasts {{date.length_hours}} hour from the start you chose.

If you have allowed it in Settings, Pilke also puts the date in your phone's calendar. The day itself is covered in [The day of the date](/en/guide/treffipaiva).
