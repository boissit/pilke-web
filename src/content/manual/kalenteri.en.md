The calendar is the **Calendar** tab in the bar at the bottom. What you mark there are date slots: times when you are up for a date.

A candidate is only found if the two of you share at least a {{calendar.min_shared_hours}}-hour stretch. The more you mark, the more people you can be a candidate for.

## The calendar

![The calendar with marked times and yellow-shaded hours](shot:kalenteri#calendar-menu,calendar-explainer)

The grid shows a few days at a time. Swipe sideways for the others, and pinch to make the hours bigger.

Beside the title are two buttons. **⋮** (1) opens **Quick actions**. **i** (2) opens a short explanation.

You can mark times up to {{calendar.horizon_days}} days ahead. Past markings cannot be changed.

## Marking a new time

![A new time being marked by dragging on the grid](shot:kalenteri-drag)

1. Press and hold the grid where the time should start.
2. Drag, and the marking grows.
3. Let go. The marking is saved.

The shortest marking the app saves is {{calendar.min_slot_hours}} h.

## Changing or removing a marking

1. Tap a marking to select it.
2. Drag it to move it, or drag its top or bottom edge to change its length.
3. Remove it with the **✕** in its corner.

A time that belongs to an invitation or an agreed date cannot be changed. The app says so: *This date slot is a date invitation, so it cannot be changed.*

## Your phone's own calendar

If you have allowed it in Settings, the events in your phone's calendar show as hatched blocks, so you can see when you are already busy. They cannot be edited in Pilke. See [Settings](/en/guide/asetukset).

## The yellow hours

Some hours are shaded yellow. Those are the times when many of the people who suit you have marked themselves free. The darker the shade, the more of them there are.

- Only people who could be your candidates count: your wishes and theirs agree, and you share a date area.
- An hour is only shaded once at least {{calendar.density_min_people}} of them are free, so nobody's calendar can be read from it.
- The shading covers the next {{calendar.density_days}} days. Days far ahead are often blank, because fewer people have marked that far.

The shade is not a headcount. It tells you when marking is most worth it if you want candidates.

## Repeating a week

![Quick actions, and a copied week proposed in the calendar](shot:kalenteri-toistuva#repeat-proposal,repeat-save,repeat-cancel)

1. Press **⋮** and choose **Repeat this week**.
2. The app copies this week's date slots, Monday to Sunday, onto the week after. The proposed times are drawn dashed, and above the grid (1) you can see how many times and hours the proposal holds.
3. You can adjust the proposed times before saving.
4. Press **Save** (2), or throw the proposal away with **Cancel** (3).

Hours already taken by a date are left out. If there is nothing to copy, the app says: *Nothing to copy this week.*

## The other quick actions

**Suggest date slots** proposes free times from your phone's calendar and the times you have marked before. It needs permission to read your calendar, and its proposal is saved the same way as a copied week.

**Clear the calendar** removes your future date slots. Agreed dates and open invitations stay. The app asks **Clear them?** before anything goes.

## Petals from your calendar

Your calendar earns petals for as long as the times in it stand. About {{calendar.petal_week_hours}} hours marked for a week earns one petal. The detail is in [Petals](/en/guide/teralehdet).
