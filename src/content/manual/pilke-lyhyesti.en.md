Pilke is built around three cornerstones.

- **Keep your calendar up to date.** Mark the times you're free for a date. Your matches are people who suit you and share time for a date with you. Other people find you on Pilke on the same terms. See [Your free times](/en/guide/vapaat-ajat).
- **Invite someone.** Find {{candidates.per_set}} matches and send one of them an invite. See [Who you're shown](/en/guide/ehdokkaat) and [Invites](/en/guide/kutsut).
- **Answer invites and go on your dates.** When someone invites you, pick a time and a place, or decline. Once it's a date, you go on it. See [The day of your date](/en/guide/treffipaiva).

![The Dates page: sparks at the top, invites and dates in the middle, Find a date! at the bottom](shot:treffit#balance-counter,find-dates)

The Dates page in the app is at the heart of it all. Your sparks are at the top (1). You earn them from your calendar and your dates, and {{petals.set_cost}} sparks send an invite. See [Sparks](/en/guide/kipinat). In the middle are the invites you've received, the ones you've sent and your upcoming dates. Below them is **Past dates**, with the dates you've been on. **Find a date!** (2) finds you new matches.

## Everything is said face to face

Pilke sets up the date, so there's no need to message. Get to know each other face to face, not by chatting.

You only really get to know someone face to face. In messages it's easy to paint an unrealistic picture of yourself, say crude things, leave the other person to carry the conversation, and so on. So Pilke takes you straight to the date.

## Signing up

Setting up your profile takes about fifteen minutes. Pilke is for people aged {{profile.min_age}} and over. While setting up your profile, you tell us about yourself, and then you take the personality quiz, which is the basis for picking your matches. Later on, your date feedback counts for more, because it's how we've got to know you and everyone else on Pilke.

- **The personality quiz.** {{profile.story_questions}} situations where you choose what you'd do. Your answers are the basis for picking your matches.
- **Who you're looking for and what you want from a date.** These narrow down your matches, and you have to fit what they're looking for too.
- **Languages.** You and your matches share at least one language.
- **Date areas and things to do.** You pick at least {{profile.min_categories}}, and Pilke picks the places for your dates from them.
- **Your free times.** You say when you'd most like to go on dates. From these, Pilke suggests free times for the coming weeks, or you can mark the times that suit you yourself. The more time you mark free, the better we can find you matches. To find a date, you need a free time of at least {{calendar.min_slot_hours}} hour.

You get {{petals.signup_bonus}} welcome sparks for confirming your phone number. That's enough for one invite, so you can invite someone as soon as your profile's ready.
