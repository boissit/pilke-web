On Pilke you do three things. Dates happen when you do all three.

- **Keep your calendar up to date.** Mark the times you're free for a date. Your matches are people who are free when you are, and other people find you the same way. See [Your free times](/en/guide/vapaat-ajat).
- **Invite someone.** Find {{candidates.per_set}} matches and send one of them an invite. See [Who you're shown](/en/guide/ehdokkaat) and [Invites](/en/guide/kutsut).
- **Answer invites and go on your dates.** When someone invites you, pick a time and a place, or decline. Once it's a date, you go. See [The day of your date](/en/guide/treffipaiva).

You'll find all of this on the Dates page. Your sparks are at the top (1). You earn them from your calendar and your dates, and {{petals.set_cost}} sparks send an invite. See [Sparks](/en/guide/kipinat). In the middle are the invites waiting for your answer, the ones you've sent and your upcoming dates. Below them is **Past dates**, with the dates you've been on. **Find a date!** (2) finds you new matches.

## Everything is said face to face

Pilke sets up the date, so there's no need to message. You show up and get to know each other.

You only really get to know someone face to face. In messages it's easy to make yourself look better than you are. Long chats also build up expectations a first meeting doesn't always live up to. So Pilke takes you straight to the date.

That makes a couple of rules strict. Your date can't ask you anything, and you can't tell them you're running late. They turn up and wait. If your date doesn't turn up, you can tell us while the date's still on. See [The day of your date](/en/guide/treffipaiva).

## Signing up

Setting up your profile takes about fifteen minutes. Pilke asks for your phone number, preferred name, date of birth and gender, and a photo. You confirm your number with a code sent by text message, and you sign in with it from then on. Pilke is for people aged {{profile.min_age}} and over. Then you answer the questions your matches are picked by:

- **The personality quiz.** {{profile.story_questions}} situations where you choose what you'd do. Your answers shape the order your matches are shown in.
- **Who you're looking for and what you want from a date.** These narrow down your matches, and you have to fit what they're looking for too.
- **Languages.** You and your matches share at least one language.
- **Date areas and things to do.** You pick at least {{profile.min_categories}}, and Pilke picks the places for your dates from them.
- **Your free times.** You say when you'd most like to go on dates, and you can connect your phone's calendar. From these, Pilke suggests free times for the next week. You check the suggestion before you save it. The more time you mark free, the better the matches we can find. One {{calendar.min_slot_hours}}-hour time is enough to start.

You get {{petals.signup_bonus}} welcome sparks for confirming your phone number. That's enough for one invite, so you can invite someone as soon as your profile's ready. Last, you can allow notifications. On **All set!** you press **Find a date**. If no matches turn up yet, **Next steps** on the Dates page tells you what you can do.
