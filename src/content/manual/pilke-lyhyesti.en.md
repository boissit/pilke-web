Using Pilke comes down to three things, and they all matter: skip any one of them and no date happens.

- **Keep your calendar up to date.** Mark the times you're free for a date. You're only matched with people who are free at the same times, and other people find you the same way. See [Your free times](/en/guide/vapaat-ajat).
- **Invite someone.** Find {{candidates.per_set}} matches and send one of them an invite. See [Who you're shown](/en/guide/ehdokkaat) and [Invites](/en/guide/kutsut).
- **Answer invites and go on your dates.** When someone invites you, pick a time and a place, or decline. Once you've agreed to a date, you keep it. See [The day of your date](/en/guide/treffipaiva).

![The Dates page: sparks at the top, invites and dates in the middle, Find a date! at the bottom](shot:treffit#balance-counter,find-dates)

The Dates page is where all three come together. Your sparks are at the top (1). Pilke runs on them: you earn them from your calendar and your dates, and {{petals.set_cost}} sparks send an invite. In the middle are the invites waiting for your answer, the ones you've sent and your upcoming dates. Below them is **Past dates**, with the dates you've been on. Press **Find a date!** (2) to find new matches. See [Sparks](/en/guide/kipinat).

## Everything is said face to face

Pilke sets up the date, so there's nothing to message about. You just show up and get to know each other.

You only really get to know someone face to face. In messages it's easy to give a polished picture of yourself, and long chats build up expectations the first meeting might not live up to. So Pilke takes you straight to the date, and getting to know each other starts there.

That's the reason for some rules that might otherwise seem strict. Your date can't ask you anything, and you can't tell them you're running late: they turn up and wait. That's why you can tell us about a no-show while the date's still on ([The day of your date](/en/guide/treffipaiva)).

## Signing up

Creating your profile takes about fifteen minutes. Pilke asks for your phone number (confirmed with a code by text message, and how you sign in from then on), your preferred name, date of birth, gender and a photo. Pilke is only for people aged {{profile.min_age}} and over. Then come the questions your matches are picked by:

- **The personality quiz**: {{profile.story_questions}} situations where you choose what you'd do. Your answers shape the order your matches are shown in.
- **Who you're looking for and what you want from a date.** These narrow down your matches both ways.
- **Languages**, so you always have one in common.
- **Date areas and things to do**, at least {{profile.min_categories}} things to do. The places for your dates are picked from these.
- **Your free times.** You say when you'd most like to go on dates, and you can connect your phone's calendar. From these, Pilke suggests free times for the next week, and you check the suggestion before saving it. The more free times you mark, the better the matches we can find. One {{calendar.min_slot_hours}}-hour time is enough to start.

When you confirm your phone number, you get {{petals.signup_bonus}} welcome sparks. That's enough for one invite, so you can invite someone as soon as your profile's ready. Last, you can allow notifications, and on **All set!** you press **Find a date**. If no matches turn up yet, **Next steps** on the Dates page tells you what you can do.
