Feedback opens once the date is over. As far as Pilke is concerned a date lasts {{date.length_hours}} hour from its start.

## Where to find it

A date that has ended moves to **Dates waiting for feedback:** on the Dates page. Tap the card.

If you have not answered yet, you get a notification at about {{feedback.prompt_hour}} o'clock the next day, if feedback requests are on in Settings.

If you told us the other person did not turn up, you are not asked for feedback on that date.

## The questions

![Date feedback: who you met, when, and the first question](shot:feedback#feedback-when)

At the top are the person you met and when (1). The questions come one at a time. **Continue** moves on and **Previous** goes back.

1. **Did your date match what you were hoping for?** Answer *No* and we ask what did not match.
2. **Did you enjoy the date?** Answer *No* and we ask what did not work.
3. **Would you like to meet your date again?**
4. **What was the place like?**

Answer honestly. The questionnaire is about your evening, not a grade for another person, and we do not tell the person you met what you answered.

You can give feedback on a date once.

## Would you like to meet again?

There are three answers:

- **Yes, and my phone number may be shared with them.** If they choose this too, we swap your numbers. They appear on the Dates page under **Numbers you have exchanged:**.
- **Yes, but only through Pilke.** If they say yes as well, you can be suggested to each other again.
- **No.**

Anything else leads to nothing at all, and neither of you learns the other's answer. To the other person, a no and no answer look exactly the same.

## If you did not feel safe

Under **What did not work?**, choosing *I felt unsafe* takes you to a report.

1. Answer the question about the place.
2. If you want to, say in your own words what happened. It is optional, and there is room for {{feedback.report_max_chars}} characters.
3. Press **Send the report**.

The report is saved and passed to Pilke's staff, and only they can see it. The two of you are never suggested to each other again.

The report does not reach the authorities. In an emergency, call 112.

## Petals for feedback

Once your feedback is sent you get {{petals.per_date}} petals. You each get your own, once you have each answered.

Back on the Dates page, a window headed **Great!** tells you so. After a report no window appears, but the petals still arrive.

If the reward would take you over the {{petals.cap}}-petal limit, it waits until you spend some. See [Petals](/en/guide/teralehdet).
