There is no messaging in the app, so nobody can say they are running late. The other person turns up and waits. That is why not turning up is the one part of a date you can tell us about while it is still going on.

## When you can report it

Only during the date:

- at the earliest, {{noshow.grace_minutes}} minutes after the agreed start
- at the latest, when the date ends. As far as Pilke is concerned a date lasts {{date.length_hours}} hour from its start.

Being a little late is not the same as not turning up, which is why the first minutes are left out.

Once the date has ended, it can no longer be reported. The app says: *The time to tell us about this date has passed.*

## Where you can report it

Only from the venue. When you press **Tell us**, your phone finds where you are and that is compared with the venue. More than {{noshow.venue_radius_m}} metres away, no report is made, and the app says: *You can only tell us while you are at the venue.*

**Your position is not stored.** It is compared with the venue at the moment you press, and it is not written down anywhere.

This check is not the same thing as showing your position on the map. You do not have to show your position to the other person in order to report, and having shown it does not help a report either.

The check needs precise location. If the app is not allowed your location, or only gets an approximate one, it says so and points you to your phone's settings.

## What a report leads to

A report is made once and cannot be taken back. After it, we do not ask you for feedback on that date.

The other person does not show up in anybody's candidates and cannot look for new ones for {{cooldown.noshow_days}} days. See [Cooldowns](/en/guide/jaahdytys).

## If a report is made about you

For as long as you can answer, the Dates page shows a notice: *The other person has told us that you did not turn up for an agreed date.* Open the date and it says **A report has been made about you**.

If you were there and did not find them, you can say that they were the one who did not turn up. On the date page, press **Tell us they did not turn up**. The same rules apply: only during the date, and only from the venue.

Once the date has ended, you can no longer answer. The date page then says *The time to answer has passed.*

## When you both say the same

The rules are the same for both of you. If each of you says the other did not turn up:

- neither of you is penalised
- a cooldown that has already started is lifted
- somebody at Pilke looks at what happened.

You both see: *When the two accounts contradict each other like this, neither of you is penalised, and a person will look at the case.*

It makes no difference which of you reported first.

## Why it works this way

A report costs the other person {{cooldown.noshow_days}} days on the word of one person. Made from the venue during the date, it describes the evening as it happened, and it cannot be made from home the next morning.
