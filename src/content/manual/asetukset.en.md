Settings is the **Settings** tab in the bar at the bottom. Its sections are stacked, and each opens when you tap its heading.

![The Settings page and its sections](shot:asetukset)

## Your details

![Your details, open](shot:asetukset-omat-tiedot#date-pace,save-own-info)

Here you change the app's **Language**, and everything candidates are found from: your preferred name, your email address, what you are looking for in a date, your languages, your **Date areas**, and what you would like to do on a date.

**How often would you like to go on a date?** (1) has three answers:

- **As often as possible:** you are always suggested to other people.
- **Once a week:** while you have an agreed date within {{matching.pace_weekly_days}} days either side of today, you are not suggested to anyone.
- **Once every two weeks:** the same, within {{matching.pace_fortnightly_days}} days.

This never stops you looking for candidates yourself.

Press **Save** (2) when you are done.

For notifications by email, confirm your address: press **Send a confirmation link** and open the link in the email.

## Security

![Security, open: the field for a trusted person's number](shot:asetukset-turvallisuus#safety-contact-input,save-safety-contact)

Save a trusted person's phone number here, and the date page shows a **Safety button**.

1. Enter the number under **A trusted person's number** (1).
2. Press **Save number** (2).

Agree it with them beforehand. The safety button texts them your name and a request to get in touch. See [The day of the date](/en/guide/treffipaiva).

## Calendar

Pilke can show your phone's calendar inside its own, and add your dates to a calendar you choose. It asks for access to your calendar when you turn one of these on.

- **Agreed dates in my calendar:** added when an invitation is accepted, removed if the date is called off.
- **Open invitations in my calendar:** added as soon as an invitation is sent or received.
- **My phone's calendar in Pilke's calendar:** the hours you are already busy show hatched while you mark your free times.
- **Add entries to:** which calendar Pilke writes to.

Your phone's calendar is only read on the phone. The entries Pilke writes appear in that calendar like any others, including in Google if you choose your Google calendar, and they name the person you are meeting.

## Notifications

![Notifications, open](shot:asetukset-ilmoitukset#save-notification-preferences)

The notifications under **Always on** always go to your phone, because they are only useful in time: **A date starting**, **When the other person arrives** and **Cancelled dates**.

For the rest, choose **Phone**, **Email**, both or neither, then press **Save choices** (1).

Switch one off entirely and the app checks with you first. Email only works while your address is confirmed; until then, the notification comes to your phone.

**A reminder about an empty calendar** comes on Sundays at about {{notifications.calendar_nudge_hour}} o'clock if nothing is marked for the week ahead.

## Account

![Account, open: signing out and deleting the account](shot:asetukset-tili#sign-out,sign-out-everywhere,delete-account)

You always sign in with a one-time code sent to your number, and signing in is per device.

- **Sign out** (1) signs you out of this device only.
- **Sign out of every device** (2) signs you out everywhere, this device included. Use it if your phone is lost or lent out.
- **Delete account** (3) deletes your account. The app asks **Delete your account?** and you confirm with **Delete**.

When you delete your account:

- your name, photo and contact details go straight away and cannot be brought back
- agreed dates still to come are called off, and the other person is compensated
- invitations you sent are withdrawn, and invitations you received are declined
- a date that is already under way is left as it is
- past dates and the feedback about them stay, and where your name was they see *Deleted user*.

You can register again later with the same number. You start from an empty account.
