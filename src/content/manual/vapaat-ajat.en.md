In the calendar, you mark the times you're free for a date. A match is someone you share at least {{calendar.min_shared_hours}} hour of free time with. It works the other way too: other people only get you as a match for times you've marked. The more time you mark, the more people you can be a match for, and the better the matches we can find.

Keeping your calendar up to date also earns sparks. See [Sparks](/en/guide/kipinat).

## What you can mark

- You can mark up to {{calendar.horizon_days}} days ahead.
- A free time has to be at least {{calendar.min_slot_hours}} hour long.
- The past is locked. A free time that has started or ended can't be changed or removed.
- Times held by an open invite or an upcoming date are locked. See [Invites](/en/guide/kutsut).

Gestures: press and hold the grid and drag to add a free time. Tap it to select it, then drag it to move it, drag the handle on its top or bottom edge to change its length, or remove it with the **✕** in its corner.

## The yellow hours

![The calendar: your free times in coral, with yellow hours near them](shot:kalenteri#calendar-density)

The yellow shading (1) shows the hours when lots of people who'd suit you are free. The deeper the shade, the more of them there are compared with the other hours on screen.

- It only counts people who could be your matches: your preferences, languages, things to do and date areas fit together.
- An hour is only shaded once at least {{calendar.density_min_people}} people are free in it.
- It covers the next {{calendar.density_days}} days. Days far ahead are often pale, because few people have marked that far.

So mark times where the yellow is: that's when you're most likely to find matches.

## Copying a week

![The calendar's Quick actions open: Copy this week at the top, the ⋮ button behind it](shot:kalenteri-valikko#calendar-menu,repeat-week)

Press **⋮** at the top of the calendar (1) to open Quick actions, then choose **Copy this week** (2). It copies the free times of the week you're looking at, Monday to Sunday, to the next week.

![The Copy this week suggestion: the suggested times drawn dashed, with a summary and the Save button above them](shot:kalenteri-toistuva#repeat-proposal,repeat-save)

The copy shows up as a suggestion first. The times are drawn dashed, and the summary (1) says how many times and hours it adds up to. Press **Save** (2) to keep it.

## Your calendar

Under Calendar in Settings, you can connect Pilke to your own calendar, such as Google or Apple Calendar. Each direction is a separate choice.

- **Pilke adds** your upcoming dates, and open invites if you like, to a calendar you choose. An entry shows your date's preferred name and, once it's decided, the place. If a date is cancelled, its entry is removed. Anyone who can see that calendar can see the entry, in Google for example.
- **Pilke reads** your plans from your calendar and shows them striped in Pilke's calendar, so you don't mark yourself free when you're busy. Only you can see your plans.
