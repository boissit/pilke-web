The Dates page is what opens when you open the app. It is the **Dates** tab in the bar at the bottom.

## What is on it

![The Dates page: petals at the top, invitations and dates below, Tip bank and Find a date! at the bottom](shot:treffit#balance-counter,tip-bank,find-dates)

At the top is your petal count (1). The rose fills as petals come in: {{petals.per_rose}} petals make one rose.

Bottom left is the **Tip bank** (2): advice on dating safely, on conversation, and on using the app.

Bottom right is **Find a date!** (3), which is how you look for new candidates. If you have opened a set and not yet sent an invitation from it, the button reads **Finish your invitation** and takes you back to that same set.

## Invitations and dates

The cards in the middle are grouped like this:

- **Date invitations you have received:** invitations waiting for your answer. Tap a card to answer it.
- **Dates waiting for feedback:** dates you have been on and not yet given feedback about.
- **Agreed dates:** dates still to come. The card opens the date page.
- **Date requests you have sent:** invitations waiting for the other person's answer.
- **Numbers you have exchanged:** dates after which you both wanted to swap phone numbers. Tap the number to send a message, or anywhere else on the card to copy it.

With nothing to show, the page says **No agreed dates**.

## Notices at the top

Two notices can appear above the cards.

If the other person has told us you did not turn up to a date, a notice says so. Tap it and the date page shows what you can do. See [Not turning up](/en/guide/saapumatta-jaaminen).

If you are not showing up in other people's candidates, or cannot look for candidates yourself, a notice says which, why, and until when. See [Cooldowns](/en/guide/jaahdytys).

## Petals

![The Petals explanation, open](shot:popover-petals)

Tap your petal count for a short explanation.

- A set of candidates costs {{petals.set_cost}} petals, which is one rose.
- Petals come from the times you mark in your calendar, and from dates you have been on once you give feedback about them.
- You can earn up to {{petals.cap}} petals, or {{petals.cap_roses}} roses. Anything earned beyond that waits until you spend some.

Petals cannot be bought. The whole of it is in [Petals](/en/guide/teralehdet).

## The feedback reward

After you give feedback on a date and come back to the Dates page, a window headed **Great!** tells you how many petals you got. Close it with **Thanks!**.

## The tour

Once you have sent your first invitation, the app walks you through the Dates page and the other tabs. Press **Next** to move on and **Got it!** to finish.
