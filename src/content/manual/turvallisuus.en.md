## The Safety button

First, save your safety contact's phone number under **Safety** in Settings. The **Safety button** then appears on the date's screen, from {{safety.lead_hours}} hours before the date starts until {{safety.trail_hours}} hours after. Agree this with your safety contact beforehand.

One tap sends them a text message, *Pilke: [your name] pressed the safety button and is asking you to get in touch.* It has nothing about your location, the place, the time or your date. Your date isn't told you pressed it. Pilke keeps a record that you pressed it and whether the message got through.

The screen tells you whether the message was sent. If it wasn't, call your safety contact yourself. In an emergency, call 112.

## Reporting that you felt unsafe

If you answer *How did the date go?* with *Badly*, we then ask *Did you feel safe on the date?* The answer *I felt unsafe* creates a report for the Pilke team. Under *Why did you feel unsafe?*, pick at least one reason. Under *Anything else*, you can also write in your own words, up to {{feedback.report_max_chars}} characters.

Only the Pilke team sees the report, and we'll read it as soon as we can.

The report isn't sent to the authorities. In an emergency, call 112.

## Telling us how your date behaved

If you answer the safety question with *Something bothered me*, you can tell us, if you like, what happened in your own words under *Tell us what happened*, up to {{feedback.report_max_chars}} characters.

Only the Pilke team sees the message you write, and we'll read it as soon as we can. Your date isn't told about it.
