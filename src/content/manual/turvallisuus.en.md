## The safety button

The **Safety button** appears on the date's screen once you have saved a trusted person's phone number under **Security** in Settings. Without a number there is no button, so save it beforehand and agree it with them.

One press sends them a text message: *Pilke: [your name] pressed the safety button and is asking you to get in touch.* It contains no location, no venue, no time and nothing about your date. Your date is not told you pressed it. Pilke records that you pressed it and whether the message got through.

The screen tells you whether it was sent. If not, call your trusted person yourself. In an emergency, call 112.

## Reporting that you felt unsafe

When you answer the feedback's *How did the date go?* with *Not well*, or choose *Something bothered me*, we ask *Did you feel safe on the date?* If you answer *I felt unsafe*, the questionnaire takes you to a report. You can pick what happened and describe it in your own words if you want to, up to {{feedback.report_max_chars}} characters. The report goes when you press **Send**, even with nothing filled in.

The report goes to Pilke's staff, and only they see it. We read it as soon as we can. Your date does not see it.

A report does not reach the authorities. In an emergency, call 112.

## Telling us how your date behaved

When your feedback says your date was very late, was on their phone a lot or was rude, or that something bothered you, we ask *Would you like to tell Pilke's staff about this?* It is up to you. If you answer *Yes, I'll tell them*, you can describe what happened in your own words if you want to, up to {{feedback.report_max_chars}} characters.

Only Pilke's staff see what you tell us, and we read it as soon as we can. Your date is not told about it.
