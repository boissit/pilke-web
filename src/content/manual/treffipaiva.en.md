Open the date from **Agreed dates:** on the Dates page. You get a reminder {{date.reminder_hours}} hour before, if that notification is on in Settings.

## The date page

![An agreed date: the time, the place, the map and the safety button](shot:date#agreed-time,venue-map)

The page shows who you are meeting and the **Time:** (1). Below that are the name of the place and what you do there.

Tap the map (2) to see the place full screen. **Route** opens directions in your phone's maps app.

## The safety button

The **Safety button** sits beside the title if you have saved a trusted person's number under **Security** in Settings. Without a number there is no button, so save one beforehand.

Pressing it sends that number a text message with your name, asking them to get in touch with you. The message does not contain your location or anything about the date, and the person you are meeting is not told you pressed it.

The screen tells you whether the message went. If it did not, call your trusted person yourself. In an emergency, call 112.

## Finding each other there

You can show the person you are meeting where you are on a map, so the two of you find each other. It is up to you.

Showing your position works from {{sharing.lead_minutes}} minutes before the start until {{sharing.trail_minutes}} minutes after. Before then, the date page says from what time you can. You get a notification when that time comes.

![The venue map and the Show my position button](shot:date-map#meet-up-reveal)

1. Go to the venue.
2. On the date page, tap the map under **Finding each other**.
3. Press **Show my position** (1).

![Show your position? and its explanation](shot:date-location-consent)

4. Read the explanation and press **Show my position**.

Your phone checks whether you are within about {{sharing.radius_m}} metres of the venue. If you are, your position appears on the map for the person you are meeting and nobody else, and they are told you have arrived.

On the map the orange pin is them, the pale one is you and the dark one is the venue. Each pin shows the last position sent.

Your position keeps updating even when you leave the screen. Showing it ends when the time runs out, or when you press **Stop showing my position**.

If you are still too far away, the app says: *You are not near the venue yet.* Showing your position needs the app to have precise location. What is stored and for how long is on [Privacy on a date](/en/guide/yksityisyys).

## If they do not come

If you are left waiting, you can tell us, during the date and from the venue.

1. Wait until {{noshow.grace_minutes}} minutes have passed since the start. **Did you end up waiting alone?** appears on the date page.
2. Press **Tell us they did not turn up**.
3. Confirm with **Tell us**.

The app checks where you are. If you are within about {{noshow.venue_radius_m}} metres of the venue, the report is recorded. Once made, it cannot be taken back.

![The report is refused because you are not at the venue](shot:date-noshow-not-at-venue)

Away from the venue, the app says: *You can only tell us while you are at the venue.*

What the report leads to is on [Not turning up](/en/guide/saapumatta-jaaminen).

## Calling a date off

If you cannot make it, call it off as soon as you know.

1. On the date page, press **Cancel the date**.
2. Confirm with **Cancel the date**.

The other person is told, and gets petals by way of compensation. For a while you do not show up in other people's candidates and cannot look for new ones yourself, and the closer the date, the longer that lasts. See [Cooldowns](/en/guide/jaahdytys).

Once the start time has passed the button is gone, and the page says *This date has already happened.*
